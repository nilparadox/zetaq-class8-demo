# Credits

- **3D models** in `models/` are light copies (fewer triangles, 512–1024 px colour texture only) of models from the owner's
  3D World Library, which were made in Tripo Studio on a paid plan (commercial use allowed, no credit line required):
  Harappan brick ruin, Harappan pottery, Harappan seals, beads / bangles / weights, grains and pulses bowls,
  Kankrej bullock, zebu calf, water buffalo, Deccani sheep, Indian pariah dog, wheat, cotton, reeds, green shrub,
  grass tuft, babul, khejri, peepal, dead tree, firewood stack, haystack, mud mound, loose stones.
  Left out on purpose after a fact-check: the rooster (chickens reached South Asia later), neem (not established for this place and date)
  and the clay lamps and handled jug (later forms).
- **three.js** r128 (MIT licence) and its GLTFLoader, loaded from public CDNs.
- **Nunito** typeface (SIL Open Font Licence), loaded from Google Fonts.
- Buildings, textures, carts, boats, kilns, music and sound effects are generated by the game's own code.
