The city is modelled on Mohenjo-daro, one of the largest cities of the Harappan (Indus Valley) civilisation. The Great Bath, great hall, pillared hall and long building follow the excavation plans. The other buildings and all the houses are typical examples, not copies of real ones. Distances are shortened and the mound is lower than the real one (about 12 m). Roofs, upper floors and the wooden hall are reconstructions: only the brick walls survive. There are no people in the city yet.
